Getting started

What do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 27 or later. To share playlists with your band, you and your musicians need to be signed in to iCloud.

How do I make a song map?

Open the Library tab, tap +, and choose an audio file. SongMap finds the chords, sections, key, tempo and lyrics on your iPhone. This takes a minute or two; you can keep using the app while it works.

How do I change chords?

Open the song and tap Edit. Tap a chord to pick a suggestion, play it on your instrument to detect it, or build it by hand. To reharmonize a few bars, select them and try AI suggestions or record new chords. Change genre recolors the whole song and lets you preview it with a band first.

Where do the genre versions go?

Every genre you apply is kept as a version. Switch between them, or back to Original, from the ♪ Original menu at the top of the song map.

How do notes work?

Tap the speech-bubble button. Voice Notes lets you say the section and your note, for example "guitar, open strumming in verse 1, bar 2", and turns it into notes. Manual Notes lets you write one note for a section, bar and instrument.

Playing with your band

How do I share a playlist?

Create a playlist in the Playlist tab, add songs, then tap Share and send the invitation. Your musicians open it on their iPhones. Any change you save later shows up for them automatically.

I'm a musician. What should I do first?

Open the shared playlist and choose your role. Songs open once a role is chosen. Your instrument is highlighted in each section, bars you don't play are dimmed, and you see the notes meant for you. You can add personal notes that only you see.

How does the stem mixer work?

Tap the mixer button next to Original. The first time, SongMap separates the song into parts, which can take a minute. Then turn vocals, keys, guitar, bass or drums up or down to focus on your part.

Troubleshooting

The shared playlist doesn't open or update

Check that both iPhones are online and signed in to iCloud, with iCloud Drive turned on. Pull down on the playlist to refresh. If the owner stopped sharing or deleted the playlist, it disappears from your list.

Chord detection or voice notes don't hear me

Go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone and turn on SongMap. For voice notes, also check Speech Recognition.

How do I delete my data?

Delete songs from the Library and playlists from the Playlist tab. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone. Shared playlists are kept in your iCloud account; you can remove them in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.
