Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

In short: SongMap does not collect your data. Your songs, song maps, notes and recordings stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account, and are shared only with the people you invite. We have no servers, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

SongMap is made by Tim Salam Toleransi ("we"). If you have questions about this policy, email otnielkalit25@gmail.com.

What SongMap stores, and where

  • On your iPhone: the audio files you import, the song maps made from them (chords, sections, lyrics, tempo, key), the separated instrument parts used by the mixer and band preview, your notes, playlists and display settings.
  • In your iCloud account: playlists you share, with their songs, song maps and notes, are stored with Apple's CloudKit in your own iCloud account so the people you invite can open them. We cannot see or access this data.

Processing on your device

Finding chords, sections, tempo and lyrics, separating instruments, chord suggestions and turning voice notes into text all run on your iPhone. Your audio and recordings are not sent to us or to any third-party service for this.

Microphone and speech recognition

SongMap asks for the microphone only when you use a feature that listens: detecting chords you play (Record Chords, Live Detection, Follow instrument) and recording voice notes. Speech recognition is used to turn voice notes into text on your iPhone. Recordings are used for that moment only; they are not saved as audio and not sent anywhere. You can turn either permission off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security.

Sharing with your band

When you share a playlist, Apple's iCloud sharing sends an invitation to the people you choose. They can see the playlist's songs, song maps and the notes meant for them, and your name as iCloud shows it. Musicians' personal notes stay on their own iPhones. You can stop sharing at any time, and deleting a shared playlist removes it for everyone.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell or share personal information.
  • We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't require an account or sign-in.

Apple services

iCloud and CloudKit are provided by Apple and covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. If you choose to share crash reports and app analytics with developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated reports. These do not identify you.

Deleting your data

Delete songs and playlists in the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your iPhone. Shared playlists in iCloud can be removed in the app, or in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Children

SongMap is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how SongMap handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
